# 🌈 Beyond The Rainbow

A tiny 2D action-platformer created for **js13kGames 2026**, built around the theme **Unicorns and Rainbows**.

A powerful curse has stripped a Rainbow Guardian of its magic and scattered its seven colours across the world. Play as the cursed black unicorn, defeat the guardians, recover the colours, unlock their powers, and face the wizard responsible.

## 🦄 Story

Once, a unicorn protected a world filled with colour and magic.

A mysterious wizard wanted the power contained within its rainbow horn. Instead of simply stealing it, he cursed the unicorn and shattered its rainbow power into **seven colours**.

Now the unicorn is weak, corrupted, and almost completely black.

The seven colours have been scattered across different worlds and placed under the protection of powerful guardians.

Recover them all, break the curse, and restore the rainbow.

## 🌈 Seven Colours, Seven Powers

| Colour | Power | Gameplay |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 Red | Fireball | Burns vines and enemies |
| 🟠 Orange | Dash | Cross long gaps and escape attacks |
| 🟡 Yellow | Lightning | Strike nearby enemies |
| 🟢 Green | Grow | Create platforms and heal |
| 🔵 Blue | Freeze | Freeze water and enemies |
| 🟣 Indigo | Teleport | Blink through thin walls |
| 🟪 Violet | Time | Slow the world down |

The seven colours aren't just collectibles — **each one changes how you play**.

## 🗺️ The Journey

1. **The Forgotten Forest** — 🔴 Red Guardian
2. **The Broken Canyon** — 🟠 Orange Guardian
3. **The Dark Caves** — 🟡 Yellow Guardian
4. **The Corrupted Garden** — 🟢 Green Guardian
5. **The Flooded Ruins** — 🔵 Blue Guardian
6. **The Void Castle** — 🟣 Indigo Guardian
7. **The Time Tower** — 🟪 Violet Guardian

After recovering all seven colours, the unicorn faces the **Wizard** in the final battle.

## ⚔️ Gameplay

**Explore → Fight → Solve → Defeat Guardian → Recover Colour → Gain Power → Repeat**

### Controls

**Keyboard**
- `A / D` — Move
- `W` — Jump / Double Jump
- `SPACE` / Mouse Click — Attack
- `K` — Use current colour power
- `E` — Switch colour power
- `1–7` — Select an unlocked colour

**Touch / Mobile**
- On-screen controls appear automatically when using touch.

## 🖤 The Curse

The curse is part of the gameplay, not just the story.

Using colour powers increases **Corruption**. As corruption rises, the unicorn begins to glitch and the screen becomes increasingly distorted.

Managing power and corruption adds a small risk/reward element while keeping the main objective simple: **recover the seven colours and break the curse.**

## 🎨 Technical Approach

This game was designed around the **13 KB challenge**.

Instead of large image assets and sprite sheets, the game uses:

- HTML5 Canvas
- Procedurally drawn characters
- Procedurally generated environments
- Reusable enemy and guardian systems
- Particle effects
- Synthesized sound
- Compact JavaScript
- Data-driven level progression

The unicorn, enemies, guardians, environments, effects, and story scenes are drawn directly with Canvas to keep the game extremely small.
